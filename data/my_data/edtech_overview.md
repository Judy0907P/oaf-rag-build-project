# What is EdTech?

EdTech, short for educational technology, is the use of software, hardware,
and digital media to support teaching and learning. The term covers a wide
range of tools: a video lecture, a flashcard app, a learning management
system used by a whole university, and an AI tutor that answers questions
at midnight are all examples of EdTech.

## A short history

Educational technology is older than the internet. Radio lessons were
broadcast to schools in the 1920s and 1930s, and educational television
followed in the 1950s. In the 1960s, the PLATO system at the University of
Illinois let students work through lessons on shared computer terminals,
and it introduced early versions of online forums and instant messaging.

The personal computer brought drill-and-practice software into classrooms
in the 1980s. The web made course materials available anywhere in the
1990s, and learning management systems became standard at colleges in the
2000s. Massive open online courses (MOOCs) appeared around 2012, promising
free university-level courses to anyone with an internet connection.

## Main categories of EdTech

- **Content delivery**: video platforms, digital textbooks, and open
  educational resources.
- **Practice and feedback**: quiz apps, coding exercises with automatic
  grading, and language-learning apps.
- **Course management**: learning management systems that organize
  assignments, grades, and announcements.
- **Communication and collaboration**: discussion boards, shared documents,
  and video conferencing.
- **Personalization**: adaptive learning systems and AI tutors that adjust
  to each learner.

## Why it matters

Good EdTech can make learning more flexible, give students faster feedback,
and help teachers see which concepts a class is struggling with. Poor EdTech
can add screen time without improving learning, widen gaps between students
with and without reliable devices, and collect more student data than it
needs. The useful question is usually not "should we use technology?" but
"does this specific tool help these specific learners learn something
better than the alternative?"
